Settings

Change how Resonate Lab sounds.

Sound effects

Short sounds play when you press a button or when something changes. On My Sounds, notes also move from your left ear to your right ear. They are off until you turn them on.

Motion

If your device is set to reduce motion, the 3D torus stays still. A dot moves around it instead. You can change this in your device settings.
