Trim controls

To mark the starting point, press T or Ctrl+T.

To mark the end point, press E.

Use the space bar or Ctrl+Space to pause or play audio.

Use the up arrow key to move 5 seconds ahead in the audio.

Use the down arrow key to move 5 seconds behind in the audio.

After entering the start and end of your trimmed audio, click enter to save.


You are now in the trimming audio player. Press Space or Ctrl+Space to play or pause. Press T or Ctrl+T to set start time. Press E to set end time.
