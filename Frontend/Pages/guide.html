Quick guide

How to turn a sound into a shape.

Three steps

Do one step at a time.

  1. 1

    Add a sound

    Choose a sound file. Press Trim if you want only part of it. Then press Save.

    Add a sound →
  2. 2

    Pick a shape

    Choose the ring or the plane. Change the size. Press Confirm, then Download.

    Pick a shape →
  3. 3

    Listen and touch

    Print your shape. Hold it while your sound plays.

    Go to My Sounds →

Ready?

Start with step 1.
