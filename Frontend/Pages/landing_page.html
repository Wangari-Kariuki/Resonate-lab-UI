Made for every hand and ear

You can use the whole app with a keyboard or a screen reader. Sound effects can help you know what happened.

See the keys

  • Keyboard first

    Tab moves you. Enter or Space presses a button.

  • Screen reader ready

    Every button and picture has a clear name.

  • Sound effects

    Short sounds tell you a button worked. They are off until you turn them on.

  • Hold your sound

    Loud parts feel tall. Quiet parts feel low.

Shapes you can make

Each shape shows your sound in a different way.

  • Sound torus

    Your sound wraps around a ring. Follow it with your finger.

  • Sound plane

    Your sound lies flat. Feel it from one side to the other.

  • Printed ring

    This is what a real print can look like.
