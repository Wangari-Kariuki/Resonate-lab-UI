Help

Keys and tips for using Resonate Lab.

Keys you can use

These keys work the same way on every page.

Read the quick guide

  • Tab

    Go to the next button or link. Shift and Tab goes back.

  • Enter or Space

    Press the button you are on.

  • Arrow keys

    Move a slider, pick a sound in a list, or turn a 3D view.

  • Home and End

    Jump a slider to the start or the end.

Trim keys

These work in the trim player on the New project page.

  • Space

    Play or pause.

  • T and E

    T marks the start. E marks the end.

  • Up and Down

    Jump 5 seconds ahead or back.

Sound effects

Short sounds tell you a button worked. They are off at the start. Use the Sound effects button at the top to turn them on.
